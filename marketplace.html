What is Mark?

The highlighter that actually works everywhere

Select any text on any webpage — a color toolbar appears instantly. Click a color. Done. Your highlight is saved permanently in Chrome, visible in the side panel, exportable to Obsidian or Notion, and there every time you come back.

✏️
One-click highlighting
Select text on any page. A toolbar appears. Pick a color. No account prompt, no loading screen — just a permanent highlight.
📄
Auto PDF highlighting
Mark intercepts .pdf links and opens them in a built-in viewer automatically. Zero clicks to start highlighting a PDF.
🎬
YouTube transcripts
A searchable transcript panel injects into any YouTube video. Select transcript text to highlight — the exact video timestamp is saved.
📓
Obsidian export
One click exports your highlights as native Obsidian callout blocks — complete with page title, domain, and timestamp.
🔒
Truly private
Everything in chrome.storage.local. No account, no server, no telemetry. Your highlights never leave your browser.
💡
How to get started
  1. Click Add to Chrome above — takes under 10 seconds.
  2. Go to any webpage and select any text — a color toolbar appears above your selection.
  3. Pick a color to save the highlight. Open the Mark side panel to see all your highlights.
  4. Use Menu → Obsidian, Notion, or Markdown to export anytime.
